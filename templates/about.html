Traceable AI Answers for Public Data

Over the course of four months in 2026, the Open Knowledge Foundation’s AI Learning Labs developed an AI assistant that lets you “talk” directly to public data using everyday language, ensuring every answer has a clear trail back to the source record.

We did it in partnership with two national governments —Brazil and Uruguay—, under the umbrella of the Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA), to become a reproducible and scalable reference for responsible and transparent AI integration with open data infrastructure.

Public data belongs to everyone, but finding the answers you need often requires navigating complex portals or mastering technical spreadsheets. Unlike standard AI chatbots that can sometimes invent facts (hallucinate), this assistant acts as a technical bridge directly to official open datasets, ensuring full data provenance and traceability. Today, this prototype applies those same proven principles to development cooperation and humanitarian resources from the International Aid Transparency Initiative (IATI). It ensures that civil society, journalists, and citizens can explore information safely, knowing every single claim is grounded in fact.
